Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you use the enquiry or contact form we ask for your name, email address, cell number (optional on the contact form), your message and your consent to be contacted.

How we use it

We use your details only to answer your enquiry or message. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Where it goes

The enquiry form works in your browser and does not send data anywhere until you choose to email us. The contact form prepares an email to Crowned by Busi, or sends it through a form service if one has been connected.

Your shopping bag

Your bag is saved only in your own browser (local storage) so it is still there when you move between pages. We do not track you and we do not use advertising cookies.

Your rights

You may ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing the address in our footer.
