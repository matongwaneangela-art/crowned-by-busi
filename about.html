  • Local care: real people in Randburg answer your messages and help with your order.

Hair Care Tips

  • Detangle gently from the ends upwards with a wide-tooth comb.
  • Wrap your hair in a satin bonnet or scarf before bed to reduce friction and frizz.
  • Wash with sulphate-free shampoo and condition the lengths, not the lace or the track.
  • Air-dry where you can, and use a heat protectant before straightening or curling.
